2 · Geometry
▸ Shear reinforcement — stirrups (EC2 6.2)
Strut angle and lever arm

The variable strut inclination method, EC2 6.2.3(2): cot θ between 1.0 and 2.5, that is θ between 45° and 21.8°. A flatter strut (θ → 21.8°) raises VRd,s and lowers VRd,max. The lever arm is taken as z = zfactor·d with zfactor = 0.9 as the usual approximation. Both apply to every stirrup row.

▸ Side cover and clear spacing (EC2 8.2)

Minimum clear distance between parallel bars or horizontal layers, EC2 8.2(2): the greatest of k1·Ø, (dg + k2) and 20 mm. Recommended k1 = 1, k2 = 5. Applies both to bar spacing within a layer and between stacked layers. The side cover is what the same check measures the bars in from each edge.
