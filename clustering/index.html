Clustering here is one-dimensional: it groups a single numeric column, and says nothing about any other column. Below 2000 values the split is the exact optimum for the chosen k; above that a fast approximation is used and the plot says so. Choosing k is a judgement — the elbow strip is a hint, not an answer.
